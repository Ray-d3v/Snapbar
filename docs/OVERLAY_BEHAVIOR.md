# Overlay behavior invariants

The Snapbar control surface is a fixed-size transparent GPUI window whose native Win32 region changes with the visible title-bar UI.

- The overlay window follows the center-safe area of the active Teams title bar. Only the defined 16 logical-pixel island drop may cross the caption bottom edge; it must not cover Teams meeting controls or content.
- `DWMWA_EXTENDED_FRAME_BOUNDS` provides the Teams frame in screen coordinates. `DWMWA_CAPTION_BUTTON_BOUNDS` limits the usable right side of the title bar.
- The fixed 30-pixel caption band reserves its top logical pixel for the DPI-scaled DWM frame outline. The visible idle surface below it is a 92 × 29 logical-pixel transparent title-bar affordance: two adjacent 46-pixel caption-style cells containing only the status dot and a 16-pixel camera glyph. Compact mode is one 46 × 29 logical-pixel camera cell.
- Idle and compact cells stay completely inside the caption band. They have no backplate, shadow, label, rounded bottom, or drop; the title-bar-colored material appears only after native hover disclosure completes.
- After the configured disclosure delay, the surface expands inside a fixed 272 × 46 logical-pixel envelope. Its top row remains transparent for the DWM outline, leaving a 272 × 45 visible island made from 29 caption-contained pixels plus the unchanged 16-pixel drop. Its material matches the representative color sampled from the visible Teams title bar, its shoulders follow a concave quarter-circle from the caption bottom (10 logical-pixel depth with a 16 logical-pixel inset), and its bottom corners use a 6 logical-pixel radius.
- Foreground text, disabled states, control backplates, and hover states switch as one luminance-derived palette: dark foregrounds on light title bars and light foregrounds on dark title bars. Semantic capture and status colors retain their meaning in both palettes.
- Convert `DWMWA_CAPTION_BUTTON_BOUNDS` from window-relative coordinates using `GetWindowRect`, then clamp against screen-space `DWMWA_EXTENDED_FRAME_BOUNDS`. Position from the maximum expanded surface and reject any placement whose caption-contained height plus the defined drop cannot contain the surface.
- The visible top anchor remains one logical pixel below the caption top throughout disclosure. The idle surface occupies the lower 29 pixels of the caption band; expansion adds the reserved 16-pixel drop and interpolates its width and root curve from the center. Capture, save, rescan, and exit actions remain on one horizontal row; no separate downward menu opens.
- The idle camera glyph and expanded capture button share one logical X anchor. Context controls may disclose around that anchor, but the primary capture target must not move horizontally across the transition.
- When the title-bar safe span is too narrow for the full strip, only the centered camera control is exposed; the compact camera remains a stateful, directly clickable control.
- The native region must be expressed in window-relative coordinates, including the client-area origin offset.
- `ClientToScreen` is bound directly from `user32` so the client origin can be converted without depending on a generated windows-rs module placement.
- The native region matches the current visible surface: a rectangular 92 × 29 idle region, rectangular 46 × 29 compact region, or square-top/curved-shoulder/rounded-bottom 272 × 45 expanded island inside the fixed 272 × 46 envelope. A one-alpha render surface keeps the transparent caption cells hit-testable; the reserved frame row and all other pixels outside the native region remain click-through so Teams keeps drawing its outline and title-bar dragging still works.
- Expansion begins only after a stable hover. Collapse occurs after the pointer remains outside the island silhouette for the configured delay.
- GPUI drives the spring for both directions. A normal run preserves its velocity, but a hover reversal starts a new spring generation from the currently published progress so velocity from the opposite direction cannot create a two-stage pause. Expansion reaches its controls in roughly 150–200 ms, permits a bounded 4.4% horizontal presentation overshoot inside the fixed 280 × 48 window, and settles by roughly 300 ms without exceeding the 16-pixel vertical drop. Painting and `SetWindowRgn` consume the same quantized progress.
- The no-activate overlay disables GPUI's default inactive-window frame interval. Animation frames therefore follow compositor-vsync opportunities instead of being capped at roughly 30 fps; the actual rate remains bounded by the active display and DWM.
- A window-level Win32 disclosure controller installed with `SetWindowSubclass` is the source of truth. It owns one explicit Collapsed / ExpandPending / Expanded / CollapsePending state machine, uses `TrackMouseEvent` / `WM_MOUSELEAVE`, and verifies the pointer with `WindowFromPoint` at timer boundaries. GPUI only renders the published mode.
- The expanded-only safety timer is a bounded fallback for missed leave messages; never restore permanent cursor polling.
- The subclass returns `MA_NOACTIVATE` for `WM_MOUSEACTIVATE`; operating the Snapbar controls must not activate the overlay or take focus from Teams.
- Collapsed, expanded, and compact dimensions come from one shared geometry source, and only the follower worker writes the native Win32 region.
- There is no click-to-pin state that can leave the strip permanently open.
- `--inline-titlebar` selects a second presentation: inside the same fixed 30-pixel caption band, the visible controls disclose on native hover from 92 × 29 to 272 × 29 logical pixels. Like the default island it reserves the top logical pixel for the DPI-scaled DWM frame outline, but it has zero island drop and a rectangular native region. The same window-level hover controller handles entry and collapse, so leaving the visible surface returns it to the collapsed 92 × 29 state.
- Inline disclosure uses a deliberately paced, slightly underdamped spring. Its centered width reveal is visibly in flight at 100 ms and reaches the full row at roughly 180 ms, while the controls cross-fade and fan out continuously from a tight center grouping instead of waiting behind a late visibility threshold. A small bounded contraction supplies the final settle without changing the title-bar-only silhouette.
- Inline disclosure clamps its spring presentation at the 272-pixel target instead of using the island's horizontal overshoot, so the full row cannot intrude into the caption-button exclusion span.
- The title-bar overlay and successful-capture flash keep `WS_EX_TOOLWINDOW` and `WS_EX_NOACTIVATE` and remain topmost relative to Teams. Release launches apply `WDA_EXCLUDEFROMCAPTURE` to both windows by default. Debug builds and explicit development launches with `--recordable-overlay` apply `WDA_NONE` to both, so disclosure motion and the white flash can be recorded without changing the shipped default.
